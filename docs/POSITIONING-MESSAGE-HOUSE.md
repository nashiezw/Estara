# ESTARA Positioning Message House

## One sentence to remember

ESTARA gives a real estate agency its own front door and keeps every opportunity moving behind it.

This sentence is the filter for homepage copy, outreach, demos, onboarding, press and sales conversations. Public messaging should lead with an agency's pain and desired outcome, not software categories.

## Audience

The primary buyer is the agency principal who cares about brand ownership, lead accountability and seller confidence. The daily user is the agent who needs faster property marketing and a clear next step without abandoning familiar communication habits.

## Three pillars

### Own your agency brand

Your buyers should land on your website and contact your team, without losing your agency inside someone else's search results.

Evidence ESTARA can show:

- Agency-branded websites and property pages.
- Agent profiles, custom domains, colours, typography and media.
- Direct, agency-owned enquiry capture.

### Nothing goes cold

Every enquiry, follow-up and viewing stays visible, with an owner and a clear next step.

Evidence ESTARA can show:

- Website and WhatsApp enquiry intake.
- Response timers, assignments and overdue actions.
- Viewing scheduling, feedback and seller reporting.
- Principal accountability measures by agent.

### One property, zero retyping

Add a property once. Reuse it across the agency website, adverts, brochures, buyer matching and seller updates.

Evidence ESTARA can show:

- One verified property record.
- Public listing and property page.
- Marketing Studio copy and PDF outputs.
- Buyer matching, viewing workflow and seller report.

## Words to use

- Your agency, your website, your enquiries.
- Nothing falls through the cracks.
- Know what needs to happen next.
- Add once. Use everywhere.
- Keep using WhatsApp; keep the work accountable.
- Verified activity and seller-ready proof.

## Words to avoid in the opening pitch

- Operating system.
- CRM.
- SaaS, multitenancy or automation platform.
- AI-powered as the primary promise.
- All-in-one without naming the concrete outcome.

These terms can appear in technical, procurement or investor conversations after the commercial value is understood.

## Thirty-second introduction

ESTARA helps a real estate agency own its online brand and stop opportunities disappearing in chats and spreadsheets. Add a property once and it powers the agency website, marketing, enquiry follow-up, viewings and seller updates. Agents can keep working through familiar channels while principals can see what needs attention and who owns the next step.

## Five-minute demo narrative

1. Start on the agency website so the prospect sees their brand outcome first.
2. Open one property record and show how the same facts power the listing and marketing output.
3. Submit an enquiry and show assignment, response timing and the next action.
4. Book a viewing, complete feedback and show the seller report.
5. Finish on the principal view: unanswered enquiries, overdue work and agent accountability.

## Objection anchors

**"We already use WhatsApp."** Keep using it. ESTARA captures the business record, ownership and next action behind the conversation.

**"We already list on portals."** Keep the reach, but give buyers a direct path back to your agency and retain your own enquiry history.

**"My agents will not use a complicated CRM."** The pilot begins with one daily loop: property, enquiry, response, viewing and seller update. Adoption is measured before wider rollout.

**"We can do this in Excel and Canva."** ESTARA removes retyping and connects the outcome of the advert to the enquiry, viewing and seller update.

## Proof rule

Do not publish a customer quote, logo, screenshot, improvement percentage or case study without written agency approval and the underlying evidence. Use raw counts beside percentages for small pilots.
