# ESTARA Agency Pilot Operating Plan

## Objective

Prove that ESTARA improves agency execution in live Zimbabwean workflows. Run one controlled cohort of 3-5 agencies for 60-90 days. The pilot is successful only when agencies operate real properties and enquiries in ESTARA and the durable records show better outcomes than the baseline period.

## Cohort

- Recruit 3-5 registered agencies with a principal sponsor and at least two active users.
- Prefer a mix of small and medium agencies, while keeping Harare as the initial operating area.
- Name one ESTARA owner and one agency champion for every pilot.
- Sign data-processing, support, and exit/export expectations before importing live records.

## Setup Week

1. Capture a 30-day pre-pilot baseline from the agency's existing records.
2. Import current properties, contacts, open enquiries, team members, and mandates.
3. Configure identity, response SLA, branches, public website, and WhatsApp Cloud API connection.
4. Train principals on the Today and Reports views; train agents on capture, response, follow-up, viewing, and feedback.
5. Complete the six activation milestones within seven days.

## Measures

The in-product pilot scorecard compares the current 30 days with the preceding 30 days:

- Enquiries received, answered, and unanswered.
- Answer rate and median first-response time.
- Due and completed response/follow-up actions.
- Enquiry-to-viewing and enquiry-to-offer conversion.
- Won deals.
- WhatsApp enquiry count and share.
- Approved seller reports and median creation-to-approval time.

Principals export the scorecard CSV from Reports at the same time each week. Qualitative notes must explain operational events, staffing changes, marketing campaigns, outages, and data-quality gaps; they must not replace the measured evidence.

## Success Gates

- At least 4 of 6 activation milestones completed in seven days; target all 6.
- At least 70% of invited active agents use ESTARA weekly by week three.
- Unanswered enquiry rate improves by at least 30% relative to baseline.
- Median response time improves by at least 25% relative to baseline.
- Follow-up completion reaches at least 80% by the final 30-day period.
- At least one seller report is approved for every active sales mandate during the pilot.
- At least 3 of 5 agencies, or 2 of 3 in a smaller cohort, choose to continue as paying customers.
- No unresolved severity-one security, tenant-isolation, data-loss, or workflow-blocking defect remains at pilot close.

These are decision thresholds, not promised outcomes. Small samples must be reported with raw counts beside percentages.

## Weekly Cadence

- Monday: principal reviews unanswered enquiries, overdue actions, quiet listings, and agent attention rows.
- Wednesday: agency champion samples five enquiry timelines for data quality and records missing next actions.
- Friday: export the scorecard, record qualitative context, and review defects and adoption with ESTARA.
- Every two weeks: observe agents using the product on their own phones and networks without coaching.

## Exit Criteria

At day 60, continue to day 90 only when usage is real and evidence is still accumulating. At close, export each agency's data, document every known limitation, compare baseline and final periods, record the commercial decision, and produce an evidence-led case study only with the agency's written approval.
