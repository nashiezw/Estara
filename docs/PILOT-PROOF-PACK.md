# ESTARA Pilot Proof Pack

## Purpose

Turn real pilot operation into evidence without manufacturing testimonials or overclaiming small samples. Create one private pack per agency and one redacted cohort summary.

## Before the pilot

- Written scope, data-processing expectations and exit/export plan.
- Thirty-day baseline export with raw enquiry and follow-up counts.
- Named agency champion and ESTARA owner.
- Approved list of users, properties and channels included.
- Separate consent choices for private measurement, internal screenshots, public screenshots, quote, logo and named case study.

## Evidence captured weekly

- Pilot scorecard CSV exported at the same weekday and time.
- Activation milestone status and weekly active users.
- Unanswered enquiry count and median response time.
- Due and completed follow-up actions.
- Enquiry-to-viewing and enquiry-to-offer counts.
- Approved seller reports and approval time.
- Product defects, outages, staffing changes and data-quality gaps.
- One observed mobile workflow, including device and network conditions.

## Case-study proof hierarchy

Use the strongest available evidence in this order:

1. Auditable before-and-after product metrics with raw counts.
2. A screenshot of the agency's live branded website or verified workflow, with permission.
3. A specific quote tied to an observed outcome.
4. A named continuation or paid-conversion decision.

Avoid generic praise such as "we love ESTARA" when a measurable operational result exists.

## Case-study structure

### Agency context

Team size, active properties, enquiry channels and the workflow problem in the agency's own approved words.

### Before

Baseline period, tools used, raw counts, response process and documented limitations.

### ESTARA workflow

What was imported, what changed, which users participated and the exact property-to-seller loop used.

### Result

Show baseline and final periods side by side. Include raw counts beside every percentage and label external influences.

### Agency voice

Use one approved, specific quote. Keep the original wording and the approval record.

### What remains

State adoption gaps, open defects and whether the agency chose to continue.

## Public release gate

No proof leaves the private pack until the agency approves the final text and every identifiable asset in writing. ESTARA must retain the source export, calculation method and approval date behind every published result.
