# ESTARA Five-Agency Listening Tour

## Purpose

Learn the language agencies use for their real problems before asking them to adopt ESTARA. This is discovery, not a disguised sales presentation. Run five conversations, preserve exact phrases and use the findings to choose 3-5 pilot agencies.

## Who to invite

- A principal or decision-maker who can describe agency performance.
- At least one working agent or negotiator from the same agency where possible.
- Agencies with active listings, real digital enquiries and enough brand pride to care about their own public presence.
- A mix of small and medium teams; avoid selecting only friendly agencies with unusually strong processes.

## Invitation

> We are preparing ESTARA for a small Zimbabwean agency pilot. I am not asking you to buy anything on this call. I would like 30 minutes to understand where listings, enquiries, follow-ups and seller updates become difficult in your agency. We will use the patterns to decide what must be solid before the pilot.

## Interview guide

Ask for a recent example before asking for an opinion.

1. Walk me through the last enquiry your agency nearly lost. What happened from first message to next action?
2. Where do new enquiries arrive today, and who is responsible for assigning or checking them?
3. How does a principal know whether an agent replied and followed up?
4. Show me how one new property becomes a portal listing, website page, social advert and brochure.
5. Which part gets retyped, delayed or delegated most often?
6. What do sellers ask you for after a listing has been live for two weeks?
7. How do you currently prove the work done on a mandate?
8. What would make agents refuse a new system after the first week?
9. What would have to improve within 30 days for the principal to pay for it?
10. Which phone, network and connectivity conditions must the workflow survive?

Do not ask whether they "like" ESTARA. Ask what they would do next, what record they would trust and what behavior would prevent daily use.

## Capture sheet

Record one sheet per agency:

- Agency, team size, branch count and principal sponsor.
- Current listing, enquiry and seller-update workflow.
- Tools used and who owns each handoff.
- Three exact problem phrases in quotation marks.
- Most recent concrete failure and its commercial consequence.
- Must-have first-week outcome.
- Adoption blockers, migration needs and training needs.
- Mobile devices, network constraints and WhatsApp setup.
- Interest in a 60-90 day pilot.
- Permission status for notes, recording, quotes, logo and screenshots. Treat each permission separately.

## Synthesis after five interviews

1. Group exact phrases under brand ownership, lost opportunities, retyping, seller confidence and adoption friction.
2. Count how many agencies raised each problem without being prompted.
3. Keep the homepage pillars only when the language repeats or the product evidence is strong.
4. Rank pilot candidates using the selection scorecard in the pilot plan.
5. Record contradictions. Do not smooth five different workflows into a fictional average agency.

## Safety and consent

Store interview notes as private business research. Do not place contact details, recordings or identifiable quotes in the public repository. Obtain written approval before publishing an agency name, logo, screenshot, quote or result.
